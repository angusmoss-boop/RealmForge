# Realmforge: Wanderer’s Rise — Development Log

_Reconstructed from the project development conversation. This is a historical design/changelog record rather than a source-control diff, so it records the intent and delivered behaviour of each release, including patches that were later superseded or corrected._

---

## V1 — Foundation

**Type:** Major foundation release

Realmforge began as a mobile-first, persistent fantasy RPG built around long-form progression rather than a short demo.

### Added
- Character creation with selectable backgrounds.
- Persistent game clock with Pause / 1× / 2× / 3× / 4× speed controls.
- Day/night cycle.
- Starting Greenvale region with multiple connected locations.
- Simulated travel between locations.
- Early random travel/world encounters.
- Character level plus independent skill levels.
- Large initial skill set covering combat, gathering, production and utility.
- Inventory and equipment.
- Basic combat.
- Enemy drops and death consequences.
- Shops and buying/selling.
- Gathering.
- Crafting.
- Quest journal and early quest chains.
- Random events with choices.
- Persistent world flags so event choices could be remembered later.
- Activity/event log.
- Autosaving.
- Manual save export/import.

### Design direction established
- Long progression.
- Skills reaching approximately level 100.
- Data-driven items, quests, enemies and locations.
- Persistent consequences.
- Modular architecture intended to survive future versions.

## V10.22 — Endurance Loop

**Type:** Skilling endurance / travel resource update

### Gathering sessions
- Mining, Woodcutting and other active work-node gathering no longer close after every successful material.
- After one material is harvested:
  - the work bar resets to 0,
  - the next remaining node charge becomes immediately available,
  - the same popup stays open.
- When the node reaches 0 charges, the interface changes to **DEPLETED** and offers Close.
- Session totals show gathered items, XP and rare finds.
- Pack-full conditions stop the session safely rather than consuming more resources.

### Gathering outcomes retained
- Butchered attempts still consume one potential resource charge and reset current work progress.
- Critical Work still doubles work progress.
- Instant Harvest remains rarer than Critical and completes the current material immediately.
- Rare skilling finds remain active.

### Dynamic Energy costs
- Active skilling Energy is no longer a flat cost for every target.
- Relevant actions now cost **1–5 Energy** depending on:
  - target/recipe level requirement,
  - the player’s level in the relevant skill,
  - equipped tool quality where applicable.
- Newly unlocked or under-tooled targets cost more.
- Mastered low-level resources and better equipment reduce Energy cost substantially.
- Gathering popup displays the Energy cost per action.

### Character Energy progression
- Base maximum Energy remains 100 at Character Level 1.
- Maximum Energy increases by **+2 per Character Level**.
- Existing campaigns gain the additional capacity during migration without losing their current Energy.
- Character level-ups grant the newly-created Energy capacity rather than fully refilling Energy.

### Passive Energy recovery
- Energy continues to recover whenever simulated world time advances.
- Passive recovery was increased slightly while remaining intentionally much slower than campfire/inn recovery.

### Travel Energy
- Travel now requires approximately **1 Energy per road minute**.
- A character must have enough Energy to begin a new road leg.
- Energy is spent progressively as the Travel Overlay advances, so turning back only costs distance already travelled.
- Existing mid-journey saves are migrated without retroactive Energy loss.
- Route-preview and travel UI expose road Energy requirements/remaining cost.
- Multi-leg journeys check Energy separately before each leg.
- If Energy is insufficient, Realmforge shows the required and available amounts rather than starting the journey.

### Save migration
- Added V10.22 migration for Energy capacity and active travel-energy state.
- Existing campaigns remain compatible.


## V10.23 — Second Wind

**Type:** Energy recovery balance patch

### Passive Energy recovery
- Increased passive Energy recovery to exactly **1 Energy every 3 simulated in-game minutes** while world time is advancing.
- Recovery is handled as discrete whole Energy points rather than hidden fractional Energy.
- Paused time gives no recovery because no simulated minutes advance.
- Recovery stops at the character's current maximum Energy.
- Time spent already at maximum Energy is not banked for an instant refill later.

### UI

### Compatibility
- Existing V10.22 saves migrate without resetting current Energy.
- Added a tiny recovery-minute accumulator so partially completed 3-minute recovery intervals survive normal saves.

## V10.25 — Into the Unknown

**Type:** Exploration system expansion

### Universal Explore action
- Explore is now available in the Actions section of every location in the game.
- Existing location action lists are preserved; Explore is appended only where it was previously missing.
- The action retains a timed exploration phase before resolving an outcome.

### Exploration became a local-adventure system
Exploring can now produce several different kinds of encounters instead of mainly awarding Exploration XP or firing a generic event:
- Local people and passing travellers.
- Existing world/random events appropriate to the current location.
- Enemy encounters.
- Traps and environmental hazards.
- Small local discoveries and resource finds.
- Very rare locked treasure chests.
- Quiet searches where nothing dramatic happens but Exploration XP is still gained.

### People encountered while exploring
- Named NPCs and generated passers can be discovered while exploring.
- The player can speak to them through the normal dialogue system rather than receiving a disconnected flavour-text reward.
- Their existing dialogue, relationships, shops, gifts and other social systems remain intact.

### Enemy exploration encounters
- Exploring can reveal a local creature or hostile enemy before combat begins.
- The player can choose to fight, observe for field research where available, or attempt to back away.
- Exploration skill improves the chance of withdrawing unnoticed.
- Failed withdrawal can cause the enemy to attack.
- Wild-location encounter tables are reused where available, with sensible low-level fallbacks in safer settled regions.

### Traps and hazards
Added location-sensitive hazards such as:
- Unstable mine/quarry ground.
- Ancient pressure mechanisms in ruins and crypts.
- Fen snares in Mirefen.
- Hunter snares in woodland.
- Improvised trip hazards elsewhere.

Trap choices include:
- Inspect and disarm using Exploration + Thieving, with equipped lockpicking tools helping.
- Take a safe longer route around the hazard.

Successful disarming grants skill XP and can occasionally yield salvage. Failure can cost Health, Stamina and Energy without killing the character outright.

### Rare exploration chests
Three persistent chest tiers were added:

**Tier I — Weathered Chest**
- Thieving level 3.
- Most common of the three, while still a rare Explore outcome.
- Rewards mainly gold and basic/local materials.

**Tier II — Ironbound Chest**
- Thieving level 8.
- Significantly rarer.
- Rewards gold, materials and a guaranteed piece of low-level equipment.
- Can occasionally contain useful lockpicking/utility supplies.

**Tier III — Gilded Strongbox**
- Thieving level 15.
- Extremely rare.
- Rewards substantially more gold, valuable materials and better equipment.
- Boss-exclusive unique items are deliberately excluded from the chest pool.

### Chest discovery scaling
- Chest discovery chance increases gently with Exploration mastery.
- The Scavenger perk also provides a small improvement where owned.
- Higher-tier chest odds remain much lower than Tier I even for high-level explorers.
- Tier III remains intentionally exceptional rather than becoming routine at high level.

### Persistent discoveries
- A discovered chest is stored against the location where it was found.
- Choosing to leave it does not destroy it.
- Returning to that location later displays a Discovered Chest card in the World page.
- The chest remains until successfully opened.

### Lock requirements
- Opening exploration chests requires the appropriate Thieving level.
- The player must carry a lockpick or lockpicking tool.
- Proper lockpicking tool sets qualify as picks; an equipped Tool Belt lockpicking tool also provides its normal control bonus.
- Consumable/basic picks can snap on failed attempts.
- If the final usable pick breaks, the chest remains at the location for a later attempt.

### Active lockpicking integration
- Exploration chests use the existing V7 active tumbler lockpicking minigame rather than a simple percentage roll.
- Higher chest tiers slightly tighten lock tolerance.
- Lockpicking attempts consume the existing lockpicking Energy cost.
- Successful chest opening awards both Thieving and Exploration XP.

### Legacy exploration progression preserved
Important older Explore discoveries were carried into the new system, including:
- Finding the Deep Galleries from the Old Greenvale Mine.
- The old map beneath Mossbound Ruins.
- Revealing the Blackthorn Camp trail from the Crossroads.
- The Captain Voss command-tent encounter where its story conditions are met.

### Statistics
New tracking includes:
- Total Explore actions.
- People encountered through Explore.
- Exploration enemy encounters.
- Traps encountered.
- Local discoveries.
- Chests found/opened.
- Tier-specific chest found/opened counts.

### Testing
- Full JavaScript syntax pass completed.
- Verified all 21 current locations receive Explore.
- Verified persistent Tier III chest discovery.
- Verified lockpicking start and chest completion/reward path.
- Verified trap outcome handling.
- Verified local discovery handling.
- Verified enemy exploration popup in a settled-area fallback case.

## V10.26 — Explorer's Pace

**Type:** Exploration balance + combat clock reliability patch

### Fixed: Explore stuck at 0%
- Traced the reported 0% Explore stall to combat clock ownership rather than the Explore progress timer itself.
- Combat stops simulated time while a battle is active, but older battle code did not remember the running speed it interrupted.
- When a battle was launched from an auto-paused Explore encounter popup, the popup correctly surrendered its pause state to combat, but victory could leave the campaign at 0× afterward.
- Battles now capture the interrupted clock state on entry and restore it after victory, successful flee or defeat.
- Deliberately paused campaigns remain paused after combat.
- Travel-started battles still defer to the V10.17 suspended-journey system so route restoration remains intact.
- A migration repair resumes the exact affected save state: a newly started Explore activity sitting at 0% with orphaned combat pause state.

- Explore now consumes Energy once when an expedition begins.
- Cost scales down with Exploration mastery:
  - Exploration Lv 1–4: 5 Energy
  - Exploration Lv 5–14: 4 Energy
  - Exploration Lv 15–29: 3 Energy
  - Exploration Lv 30+: 2 Energy
- The Explore action card shows the current cost before starting.
- Insufficient Energy produces a clear exhaustion message rather than starting an expedition that cannot progress.
- Exploration Energy spending contributes to the existing total Energy-spent statistic and a dedicated Explore Energy-spent counter.
- Passive recovery remains +1 Energy every 3 in-game minutes, so part of the cost naturally returns while an expedition is underway if simulated time is running.

### Design intent
- Early exploration should feel physically meaningful without competing with Travel's much larger Energy demand.
- Experienced explorers become more efficient at route-finding, climbing and searching, giving Exploration levels a small quality-of-life progression benefit beyond encounter outcomes.

## V10.27 — Locks & Locality

**Type:** Lockpicking overhaul + exploration locality update

### Circular active lockpicking
- Replaced exploration chest lockpicking with a dedicated circular active minigame.
- The presentation is deliberately closer to the feel of a circular fantasy lock mechanism while retaining Realmforge's timing-based interaction style.
- A pick rotates around the lock face and the player turns the lock while it is inside a highlighted binding arc.
- Thieving level improves control modestly when above the lock's requirement.
- Difficulty is visibly different by chest tier:
  - Weathered Chest: broadest timing arc and slowest movement.
  - Ironbound Chest: narrower arc and faster movement.
  - Gilded Strongbox: tightest arc and fastest movement.
- Older fixed lock sites also use the new circular minigame.

### Chest failure and permanent lock damage
- Each failed chest lock attempt now always breaks one physical Lockpick.
- Chest failure count persists if the player leaves and returns later.
- First failure: chest remains available with 1/3 failures recorded.
- Second failure: chest remains available with 2/3 failures recorded.
- Third failure: the mechanism jams permanently and the discovered chest is lost.
- Added statistics for failed lock attempts, broken lockpicks and permanently jammed exploration chests.

### Stackable consumable Lockpicks
- Lockpicks are now ordinary stackable utility items carried in the Pack.
- Lockpicks are no longer Tool Belt equipment.
- Removed the Lockpicking slot from the Tool Belt once no lockpicking tools remain in game data.
- Existing equipped lockpicking sets are unequipped automatically during migration.
- Legacy Fine Lockpick / Fine Lockpick Set / Masterwork Picks are converted into stacks of ordinary Lockpicks so existing campaigns do not lose value:
  - Fine Lockpick: 2 Lockpicks.
  - Fine Lockpick Set: 4 Lockpicks.
  - Masterwork Picks: 8 Lockpicks.
- Old rewards or shop stock that still reference obsolete pick types are transparently converted to normal Lockpicks.

### Smithable Lockpicks
- Added **Forge Lockpicks** to Smithing.
- Requires Smithing level 4.
- Costs 1 Iron Bar.
- Produces 3 stackable Lockpicks.
- Uses the normal active forge/smithing system rather than an instant craft.
- In Greenvale it follows the existing Pack-first, then Bank material-consumption rule.

### Burglary lockpicking
- The burglary "Work the lock" route no longer resolves through a hidden success percentage.
- It now launches the circular active lockpicking minigame.
- Every failed burglary lock attempt breaks one Lockpick.
- Repeated noisy failures gradually increase the chance that the household notices the attempt and adds bounty.
- Successful lockpicking proceeds into the existing burglary interior/loot stage.
- The three-failure permanent-jam rule applies to discovered exploration chests only, not ordinary burglary doors.

### Persistent chests moved into Actions
- Removed the separate Discovered Chest panel introduced in V10.25.
- A chest that has been discovered but not opened now appears as its own action inside that location's **Actions** grid.
- The action shows:
  - Chest tier/name.
  - Required/current Thieving level.
  - Current Lockpick count.
  - Persistent failed attempts out of 3.
- Leaving the location does not move the chest elsewhere.
- Returning later to the same location restores the chest action until the chest is opened or its lock is permanently jammed.

### Exploration locality
- Exploration enemy encounters now use the current location's actual encounter table or a small curated location-specific fallback table.
- Removed broad regional enemy fallbacks that could surface creatures in implausible places.
- All 21 current locations have a valid local Explore enemy pool.
- Local material/item discoveries now use only resources physically associated with the current location plus a small curated location-specific list.
- Enemy drop tables are deliberately not merged into local-search loot because roaming creatures can carry trophies that are thematically wrong for the surrounding terrain.
- Examples:
  - Whisperwood searches produce forest resources such as logs, herbs and berries.
  - Mine/quarry searches produce ores and coal relevant to those sites.
  - Mirefen searches produce Redroot, Alder, Bog Iron, Mooncaps, Reed Pike and other local resources depending on the exact location.
  - Settled locations such as Greenvale Mill, Eastwatch Tower and Wayfarer Hall use their own curated everyday finds.
- Chest materials follow the same local resource pools, while equipment rewards use region-appropriate gear pools and still exclude boss-exclusive unique equipment.

### Trap interaction cleanup
- Trap disarming continues to use Exploration + Thieving.
- It no longer references an equipped Lockpicking Tool Belt set because lockpicks are now consumables rather than tools.

### Save migration
- Existing V10.25/V10.26 persistent chest finds gain a `failures` counter starting at 0 if none existed.
- Existing legacy lockpicking equipment in Pack or Bank is converted safely into ordinary Lockpick stacks.
- Any old Lockpicking Tool Belt assignment is cleared.

### Testing
- Full JavaScript syntax pass completed across all 49 JavaScript files.
- Verified legacy lockpick conversion and removal of the Tool Belt lockpicking slot.
- Verified Smithing recipe data: 1 Iron Bar → 3 Lockpicks at Smithing 4.
- Verified all 21 locations have non-empty, location-relevant enemy and local-find pools.

- Verified successful chest pick opens/rewards the chest without consuming a pick.
- Verified three deliberate chest failures consume exactly three Lockpicks, persist the failure count and permanently remove/jam the chest on failure 3.

## V10.29 — Four Pins

**Type:** Lockpicking minigame overhaul / visual clarity patch

### Four-stage lock mechanism
- Circular lockpicking now requires four successful pin/ridge sets to open a lock.
- Progress is shown explicitly as 0/4, 1/4, 2/4, 3/4 and open.
- Every successful set moves the green binding target to a different point around the cylinder.
- A missed turn does not erase pins already set during the current session.

### Difficulty scaling
- Simple locks rotate faster than the original V10.27 implementation but retain a comparatively broad target.
- Hard locks rotate faster and use a narrower, thinner target band.
- Master locks rotate fastest and use the narrowest/thinnest target.
- Extra Thieving mastery gives a modest assistance without removing the tier differences.

### Visual clarity
- Rebuilt the green target arc to be substantially brighter with a visible glow.
- Added a live green highlight when the moving pick is physically inside the valid hit zone.
- Corrected a 180-degree visual-reference mismatch between CSS conic-gradient angles and the rotating pick's transform orientation, so the displayed target now corresponds to the actual success calculation.
- Added rotation-speed readout and four-pin progress indicators.

### Failure rules retained

## V10.32 — Clean Run

**Type:** Lockpicking difficulty / burglary-risk update

### Four consecutive pins required
- Lockpicking now requires all four pins to be set consecutively.
- Any failed timing attempt resets current lock progress to **0/4**.
- A successful pin still moves the green target to a new position for the next pin.

### Passive missed-window detection
- The rotating pick can no longer safely circle past a target while the player waits for another lap.
- Entering and then leaving the active green window without pressing **Turn the Lock** counts as a failed pick.
- A passed window:
  - Breaks one Lockpick.
  - Advances the lock failure counter.
  - Resets the current four-pin streak to 0/4.
- The next target is reseeded away from the current pick position so a newly reset pin begins with a fair approach.

### Manual misses
- Pressing **Turn the Lock** outside the green target now follows the same streak rule.
- Any previously set pins are lost and the sequence returns to 0/4.

### Exploration chest jams
- Existing chest rules are preserved.
- Three total failed picks permanently jam the chest.
- The discovered chest is then lost from that location.

### Burglary door jams
- Greenvale burglary doors now track lock failures out of 3 during the active break-in.
- On the third failed pick, if the player has not already been detected:
  - The door lock jams.
  - The burglary attempt immediately ends.
  - The property’s existing 24-hour cooldown is refreshed from the jam.
  - The building becomes available again once that cooldown expires.
- Earlier failures already make the property alert, so abandoning a partially failed lock cannot be used to reset the burglary target.

### UI
- Burglary lock screens now show **Lock failures X/3** rather than an open-ended quiet-failure count.
- Instructions explicitly explain the four-consecutive-pin rule and that letting the green window pass also counts as a failure.

### Save migration
- Existing saves migrate to V10.32 automatically.
- If the game is updated while a lock is already open, the current pin streak resets to 0/4 and the new timing rule is applied safely.

## V10.53 — Detached Loadout

**Type:** Inventory / equipment architecture update

### Core loadout model
- Worn **Equipment** and **Tool Belt** tools are now stored outside normal Pack inventory.
- Equipped copies therefore no longer consume Pack stack slots and no longer appear as carried Pack tiles.
- Existing saves receive a one-time migration that removes one matching Pack copy for every currently equipped gear/tool reference while preserving the loadout itself.

### Equipping and replacement
- Equipping a weapon, armour piece, ring or other worn item removes one copy from the Pack and places it into the Equipment loadout.
- Equipping a Tool Belt tool likewise removes that copy from the Pack and stores it in the matching Tool Belt slot.
- If the destination slot already contains another item, the old equipped item is automatically returned to the Pack as the replacement occurs.
- Swaps are capacity-aware: if the outgoing item cannot fit back into the Pack after the incoming item is removed, the swap is blocked instead of losing or overflowing equipment.

### Unequipping
- Manual unequip now explicitly returns the item to the Pack.
- Unequipping is blocked when it would require a new Pack stack but the Pack is already full.
- Dedicated Equipment and Tool Belt pages remain the primary place to access detached loadout items.
- Loadout item detail cards now state that the equipped copy does not consume a Pack slot and provide a **Return to Pack** action.

### Pack / Bank integration
- Pack slot usage now naturally counts carried stacks only.
- The main Pack grid no longer marks a carried duplicate as equipped merely because another copy of the same item exists in the loadout.
- Vault depositing and Quick Stack now treat every actual Pack copy as movable; equipped copies are no longer represented inside Pack inventory and therefore require no protected quantity.
- Packed duplicate copies of an equipped item can be banked or dropped independently.

### Tool Belt compatibility
- Updated the legacy V10.3 Tool Belt migration so a valid equipped tool remains equipped even when there is no matching Pack copy.
- Active skill tool lookup now reads directly from the Tool Belt loadout rather than requiring the tool to also exist in Pack inventory.
- This older-file compatibility update is required so detached Tool Belt tools survive future reloads correctly.

## V10.55 — Loadout Balance

**Type:** Combat equipment balance / backward-compatibility update

### Equipment totals now use the full loadout
- Rebuilt the live equipment-stat calculation so **all 8 combat Equipment slots** can contribute to both offensive and defensive totals.
- `Damage Output` now sums every equipped item's `damage` bonus rather than reading only the Main Hand weapon.
- `Armour` now sums every equipped item's `armor` bonus, including **Off Hand**, rings and weapons that carry a defensive contribution.
- This directly fixes shields such as **Bronze Buckler** not contributing their listed armour to the player's actual armour total.
- Existing Defence-level passive armour and the **Bulwark** perk remain part of effective Armour.
- V10.7's increased armour effectiveness while resolving enemy attacks is preserved.
- Enemy threat previews were rebound to the new complete armour calculation so displayed threat and real mitigation use the same loadout.

### Equipment stat philosophy
- Normalised all **28 current combat-equipment items**.
- Weapons remain damage-led, but suitable weapons can now carry a smaller armour bonus representing reach, guard, weight or defensive leverage.
- Armour remains armour-led, but suitable armour can now carry a smaller damage bonus representing shields, reinforced striking surfaces, mobility or combat utility.
- Existing primary progression values were deliberately preserved wherever possible, so established Attack / Defence / Archery requirements do not unexpectedly jump for ongoing campaigns.
- Examples after V10.55:
  - **Iron Sword:** +8 damage / +2 armour.
  - **Bronze Buckler:** +2 damage / +3 armour.

## V10.56 — Overburdened

**Type:** Inventory safety / encumbrance / save-compatibility update

### Lossless Pack overflow
- Pack capacity is now a **soft limit for item acquisition** rather than a destructive hard stop.
- Legitimately acquired items are never discarded just because they create a new stack while the Pack is full.
- The Pack may temporarily display values above capacity, such as **32/31 slots**.
- This applies to normal loot/rewards, gathering outputs, crafting outputs, purchases and legacy/private trade intake paths.
- V8.2's old behaviour of silently routing/discarding brand-new overflow stacks is superseded: newly acquired items now enter the Pack first so the player can decide what to keep.

### Over-encumbered state
- A character becomes **Over-Encumbered** only when Pack stack usage is above the current level-scaled capacity.
- Being exactly at capacity remains valid.
- While Over-Encumbered:
  - Simulated world time is forced to **Pause**.
  - 1× / 2× time cannot be resumed.
  - New travel cannot begin.
  - An active journey is paused safely instead of losing route progress.
  - Time-costing activities such as batch crafting, cooking, excavation, research, lockpicking and pickpocketing cannot be started until the Pack is sorted.
- Once the Pack returns to capacity or below, the player can resume time normally. Time does not auto-resume behind their back.

### Pack warning UI
- The main Pack page now shows a prominent red/orange **OVER-ENCUMBERED** warning whenever the Pack exceeds capacity.
- The warning shows:
  - Exact Pack count.
  - Exact number of excess slots.
  - Clear instructions to bank, drop, sell, use or equip items until capacity is restored.
- The Pack count is highlighted while overloaded.
- Time buttons receive a visibly locked treatment while overloaded but remain tappable so Realmforge can explain why time cannot resume.

### Travel recovery while overloaded
- If road loot or an encounter pushes the Pack over capacity during an existing journey, the journey record is preserved at its exact progress.
- The normal travel overlay is temporarily suppressed while overloaded so the player can access Pack management instead of becoming trapped behind the road interface.
- Once the Pack is legal again, the normal paused travel overlay returns and the player can resume the journey manually.

### Equipment / Tool Belt migration safety
- V10.53's detached Equipment and Tool Belt system is now explicitly overflow-safe.
- Replacing or unequipping an item may return the old loadout item to an already-full Pack instead of blocking the action or risking item loss.
- A future migration that absolutely must invalidate or remove a loadout item can use the new overflow-safe `forceLoadoutItemToPack` helper.
- This means a future rebalance can safely create temporary states such as **31/30** while preserving every item.
- Valid current Equipment and Tool Belt selections remain untouched by V10.56 migration.

### Gathering, crafting and trading
- Continuous gathering no longer refuses a reward because the Pack is full.
- Craft batch maximums are now limited by skill/materials rather than output Pack capacity. Any resulting overflow triggers the encumbrance lock.
- Market and legacy/private purchases can likewise create temporary overflow instead of making an item unavailable solely because a new stack would exceed capacity.
- Bank withdrawals retain their normal safety rules, so storage cannot be used to deliberately flood the Pack with arbitrary new stacks.

### Offline-time safety
- Updated `main.js` so an already-overloaded campaign does **not** receive offline catch-up world time before the accumulated V10.56 patch stack finishes loading.
- Such saves load paused until Pack capacity is restored.

### Existing-save / friends' campaign compatibility
- Save schema advanced to `10.56.0`.
- Existing Equipment IDs remain equipped.
- Existing Tool Belt IDs remain equipped.
- Existing Pack and Bank contents remain intact.
- V10.53 detached-loadout migration is still invoked idempotently for older campaigns jumping directly to this build.
- No valid loadout item is automatically unequipped by this update.
- If future migrations must move gear back into Pack, temporary overflow is now the required safe fallback instead of deletion or migration failure.

## V11.5.2 — Fieldcraft Research
**Type:** Field Research balance / progression integration

### Changed
- Field Research now scales with both **Exploration** and **Hunting**.
- Exploration is the stronger contributor to **safe observation**, reducing the chance that an entity notices the player and attacks.
- Hunting is the stronger contributor to **research insight**, increasing the chance that a safe observation produces **2 notes instead of 1**.
- Both skills contribute to both effects, so mixed fieldcraft builds are rewarded without making either skill mandatory.
- Safe observations always award at least one research note; low-level characters therefore still make reliable progress.
- Research danger has a **4% floor**, so even master field researchers cannot make wild observation entirely risk-free.
- Keen insight is capped at **35%**, preventing high skills from trivialising the 3-rank identification loop.
- Excess notes now carry into the next research rank rather than being silently discarded when a rank is completed.

- The Field Research panel now explains the role of Exploration and Hunting.
- The panel displays the current approximate **safe-observation percentage** and **keen-insight chance**.
- Unidentified entities now display their current note progress toward the next research rank.

- Existing research ranks and note counts are preserved exactly during migration.
- Existing Equipment, Tool Belt, Pack, Bank, dungeon, quest and world-progression state is untouched.

- Provoke chance: `max(4%, 22% - Exploration×0.5% - Hunting×0.15%)`.
