ARROW DESIGN NOTE
The existing Realmforge Arrow item remains the ammunition object. V12.1 expands how arrows are produced but deliberately does not introduce tiered arrow combat stats/consumption as a hidden mechanic change. A future ranged-combat/ammunition overhaul can own that explicitly if desired.

BALANCE CEILING
Crafted Stariron is intentionally powerful but below the strongest Legendary dungeon/relic equipment. This preserves rare-drop and dungeon value while giving production skills a dependable high-end progression route.
