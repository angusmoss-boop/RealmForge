REALMFORGE V12.3.0 - DATABASE KNOWLEDGE PROVENANCE

Purpose
Turn Living Codex detail pages into production-aware reference pages and make record ordering deterministic for the player.

Recipes & Uses
- Final owner: js/ui/database_modern.js
- Ordinary recipe source: RF.DATA.recipes
- Camp/home cooking source: RF.DATA.campRecipes
- Ordinary recipe membership is detected from each recipe's `inputs` object.
- Camp recipe membership detects the primary `input`/`qty` pair and `extra` ingredients.
- Uses are de-duplicated before display.
- Uses are sorted alphabetically by crafted output.
- Each rendered use exposes crafted output, recipe name, skill/discipline level and inspected-ingredient quantity.
- Fletching continues to be inferred from Crafting recipes that output bows/ammunition, preserving the existing skill model.
- Item entries with zero downstream recipe uses receive no empty section.
- Resource entries resolve their yielded item and show that item's downstream uses.

Example verified
Greenleaf Herb resolves live uses including:
- Minor Healing Potion — Herblore Lv 2 — 3 x Greenleaf Herb
- Antivenom — Herblore Lv 6 — 1 x Greenleaf Herb
- Focus Draught — Herblore Lv 10 — 2 x Greenleaf Herb
- Smoke Bomb — Crafting Lv 6 — 1 x Greenleaf Herb
- Venison Stew — Cooking Lv 4 — 1 x Greenleaf Herb

Camp recipe coverage
- Primary camp ingredients are included.
- Quantity fields are respected.
- Optional/extra ingredients are included, e.g. Wild Berries used by Marsh Stew.

Alphabetical contract
- Filtering/searching occurs before sorting.
- Final records are sorted A-Z by displayed name.
- Applied to Items, Resources, Recipes, Enemies, NPCs, Locations and Dungeons.
- Applied to All and every Item/Resource/Recipe refinement category.
- Bestiary Research 3/3 eligibility remains unchanged and is sorted only after eligible records are selected.

Regression protection
`tests/database_v1230.js` asserts:
- all V12.2/V12.2.1 filter/search/responsive contracts remain present;
- every populated Database sector is alphabetical;
- every refinement category is alphabetical;
- Greenleaf recipe graph contains the expected production results;
- ingredient quantities/skill levels are correct;
- camp and extra ingredients participate;
- item details place Recipes & Uses after Known sources;
- harvest-resource details inherit yielded-item uses;
- unused final products do not receive an empty Uses block.

Persistence / compatibility
- Save schema remains 11.5.3.
- No migration required.
- Frozen compatibility runtime was not edited.
