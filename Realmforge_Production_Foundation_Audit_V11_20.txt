AUDIT CONCLUSION
The safest V11.20 extraction boundary was not another wholesale compatibility patch. The remaining browser/AAB risk lived in the legacy-base startup IIFE and direct lifecycle/native-browser calls spread across startup, Travel recovery, save fallbacks and modern haptics. Moving those mechanics behind canonical Core/Platform boundaries removes native packaging assumptions without duplicating Travel Clock Sentinel or Time/Energy ownership.

EXTRACTION / PLATFORM SCOPE
- New canonical core.lifecycle owns base startup, offline catch-up, initial render/frame launch, service-worker bootstrap and save import/export shell affordance installation.
- platform.browser gains monotonic time, prompt/alert, haptics, visibility and resume/focus/pageshow adapter operations.
- Travel Clock Sentinel keeps semantic ownership but subscribes to lifecycle through RF.Platform.
- Canonical save fallback dialogs route through RF.Platform.
- Inventory, Equipment and Combat haptic calls route through RF.Platform.
- Historical execution position and gameplay formulas are preserved.
