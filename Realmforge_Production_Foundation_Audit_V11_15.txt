Canonical Fieldcraft substrate
------------------------------
js/systems/fieldcraft.js now owns the proven production-stage representation of:
- V5 Hearth & Harvest
- V6 Hands On
- V10.24 Hearth & Home

It also owns the V7 fieldcraft-only fragments:
- rare skilling
- marsh cooking
- camp extras

The V5/V6 implementation is intentionally preserved rather than rewritten wholesale because it interleaves several mature, order-sensitive fieldcraft behaviours. Compatibility now delegates those historical boundaries to the canonical owner.
