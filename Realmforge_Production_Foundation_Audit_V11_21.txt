WHY V2 MOVED AS A WHOLE STAGE
The transformed V2 stage is the historical Living World substrate. It establishes NPC schedules, world pulse/news/markets/reputation and early integration hooks as one chronological unit. Later mature systems supersede/enrich its old Shop/Combat/Travel-facing wrappers. Keeping the exact stage source together under systems.world is safer than rewriting or artificially splitting this foundational wrapper chain.

WHY V4 REMAINS PARTLY IN COMPATIBILITY
V4 is genuinely mixed. Its tactical combat engine, enemy encounter mechanics, combat specialization, bounty modal branch, Property/UI pieces and mixed binding wrapper do not belong to Social. V11.21 therefore moves only exact audited social fragments and leaves the rest in chronological compatibility.
