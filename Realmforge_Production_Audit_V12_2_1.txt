REALMFORGE V12.2.1 - PRODUCTION AUDIT

SCOPE
Mobile-width regression in Living Codex category refinement.

ROOT CAUSE
The V12.2 `.db122Chips` control was a non-wrapping horizontal flex rail. Inside the Codex CSS grid, its accumulated minimum-content width could expand the grid item beyond the mobile viewport. The screenshots supplied from Samsung showed the sector grid and workbench clipped horizontally on Items/Resources/Recipes, the three sectors that expose refiners.

FIX
- `.db122Chips`: `flex-wrap: wrap`; no horizontal overflow rail.
- Codex layout containers: `min-width:0`, `max-width:100%`, `box-sizing:border-box`.
- Direct Codex children: `min-width:0`.
- Refine hint updated from swipe-oriented copy to non-scroll category copy.
