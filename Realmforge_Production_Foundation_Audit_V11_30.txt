ANDROID / V12 READINESS
The shared web/game architecture is READY TO BEGIN V12.

It is not yet directly Google Play publish-ready because native packaging is deliberately V12 work. Required native tasks include a Capacitor/Android project and package ID, an Android RF.Platform adapter, native update/service-worker strategy, real icons/splash/store assets, Gradle/SDK and signing configuration, AAB generation, physical-device persistence/update testing, and Play Console release/policy work.

Recommended opening: V12.0 Android Foundation.
